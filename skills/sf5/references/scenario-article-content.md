# SF5 Scenario: Article Content

## Use When

- long-form article page
- editorial or knowledge-base content
- page with hero, article body, TOC, and related posts
- content page where reading flow is more important than app controls

## Main Building Blocks

- article hero
- table of contents sidebar
- article body with headings, lists, quotes, and callouts
- related content grid
- optional share/back actions

## Primary References

- `references/page-recipe-article.md`
- `references/pattern-playbooks.md`
- `references/source-inventory.md`

## Notes

- Prefer semantic article markup over dashboard or listing structures.
- Do not treat `table of contents` as a data-table intent.
- Keep the related-content area secondary to the main reading flow.
